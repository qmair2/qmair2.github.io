About Me

I am a senior at Duke University studying Electrical and Computer Engineering, with a particular interest in computer hardware, digital design, and solid-state devices. In school, I've designed and deployed a 32-bit pipelined processor on an FPGA to run a video game, and outside the classroom I've worked across controls, hardware product development, and international engineering projects. This combination of experiences has made me especially interested in work at the intersection of engineering and people, where I could combine technical skills with teamwork and ideation.
